# Mohammad Khursani (Sani)

**AI Research Lead · LLM Pretraining · Speech & Multimodal AI · Data & Evaluation**

I lead AI research and engineering in Malaysia, working across the model-development lifecycle: **data curation, training, evaluation, optimization, and production deployment**.

My strongest hands-on background is in **speech AI (ASR/TTS)** and Malay-language modeling. I am also interested in the broader problems behind capable models: pretraining data quality, efficient training, reliable evaluation, and how models adapt without losing prior capabilities.

## Selected public work

| Project | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- |
| [synthpre-557m](https://huggingface.co/khursani8/synthpre-557m) | An **experimental 557M-parameter Malay language model** with training and data methodology documented in its model card. The released checkpoint is undertrained and is shared as research work, not a production-ready model. |
| [BFCL-Malay (bfcl-ms)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/khursani8/bfcl-ms) | A Malay adaptation of a function-calling benchmark: **5,011 evaluation entries across 21 categories**, retaining compatibility with the original evaluation structure. |

| [MYChatGPT](https://github.com/khursani8/MYChatGPT) | Malay instruction-tuning dataset experiments, translated data, and notes from exploring LLM fine-tuning. |
| [MalayASR-iOS](https://github.com/khursani8/MalayASR-iOS) | An on-device Malay ASR application prototype combining Qwen3-ASR, voice activity detection, and an iOS interface. |

## Research & engineering interests

- **Pretraining and data:** corpus curation, filtering, deduplication, tokenization, training experiments, and dataset quality.
- **Model adaptation:** fine-tuning, parameter-efficient adaptation, optimizer behavior, training stability, and catastrophic forgetting.
- **Speech and multimodal systems:** multilingual/code-switched ASR, TTS, streaming inference, low-latency conversational AI, and full-duplex interaction.
- **Evaluation:** task-specific benchmarks, controlled ablations, error analysis, and measuring trade-offs rather than reporting a single score.
- **Infrastructure:** PyTorch, Hugging Face, multi-GPU training, Docker, and model serving with vLLM.
